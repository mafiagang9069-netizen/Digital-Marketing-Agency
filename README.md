# 01 — Signal & Scale | Modern Digital Marketing Agency Template

A high-contrast Swiss-grid digital marketing agency landing page built with clean **HTML5**, **CSS3**, and **Vanilla JavaScript** by **PixelNestByNS**.

---

## Design Direction
- **Style:** Modern Swiss-Grid & High-Contrast Tech Editorial with Bento Cards
- **Color Palette:** Paper `#F7F7F4`, Ink Black `#111210`, Electric Lime `#C8FF32`, Concrete `#ECECE6`
- **Typography:** `Syne` (Display), `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Body), `JetBrains Mono` (Labels & KPIs)

---

## Included Sections & Features
1. Announcement top strip & sticky blurred navigation bar with mobile drawer
2. Asymmetric Split Hero with floating campaign KPI card & **Interactive ROAS Estimator**
3. Channel capabilities strip
4. 4-Card Bento Services Grid with progress bars
5. 01–04 Growth Methodology grid
6. Visual Case Studies with metric breakdowns (sample template figures)
7. Retainer Pricing with **Monthly / Quarterly 15% Toggle**
8. Sample Testimonial placeholder + Interactive FAQ Accordion
9. Validated Growth Audit form + Footer with `Website Template by PixelNestByNS`

---

## Quick Setup
1. Double-click `index.html` to preview locally in any browser.
2. Open `index.html` in a code editor and replace headlines, brand mark (`S/S`), sample stats, and placeholder quote.
3. Update CSS variables in `style.css` (`:root`) to swap colors or Google Fonts.
4. Replace images inside `assets/`:
   - `assets/hero-marketing-studio.jpg`
   - `assets/case-study-ecommerce.jpg`
   - `assets/case-study-saas.jpg`

---

## Connecting the Form (Static HTML)
The audit form includes client-side validation in `script.js` (Demo Mode). To receive real emails:
- **Formspree:** Set `action="https://formspree.io/f/YOUR_ID"` on `#auditForm` and follow the fetch snippet documented in `script.js`.
- **Netlify Forms:** Add `data-netlify="true"` to `#auditForm`.
